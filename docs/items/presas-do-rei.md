# Presas do Rei

## Type

Dagger

## Rarity

Legendary

## Lore

Forged from the remains of the Scorpion King, the Presas do Rei carry the essence of desert venom and speed.

Every strike injects corrupted poison into the enemy.

## Gameplay Identity

- High attack speed
- Poison stacking
- Critical strike focused
- Agile assassin playstyle
